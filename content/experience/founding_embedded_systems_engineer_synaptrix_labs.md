### Description

* Designing an edge-inference BCI control pipeline for wheelchairs: intent classification from streamed EEG drives the wheelchair's motor driver actuation, with a concurrent serial channel that shows classifier state on a monitoring tablet.

* Architected a 24-channel EEG BCI front-end platform on Zephyr, streamed over BLE with sub-30 ms sensor-to-host latency.

* Delivered signed OTA firmware updates over BLE, with LE Secure Connections bonding that gates management access, and extended the platform to wrist stimulation wearables for wheelchair users.

* Built a custom Zephyr bootloader for nRF52840 with dual-slot image management and USB DFU, with reset-aware logic that switches between DFU and app execution.
